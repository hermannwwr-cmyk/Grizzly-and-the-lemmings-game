# Grizzly and the Lemmings 🐻🍯

A set of gentle, colorful mini games for young kids (about age 6), starring Grizzly the bear
and a bunch of cheeky lemmings. Pick a game from the menu by tapping its big card; swipe sideways or use the ◀ ▶ arrows to see more games.

| Game | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Catch** | Move Grizzly to catch falling honey, berries, apples and fish. Bump the sneaky lemmings so they drop the treats they steal. 3 levels. |
| **Boop!** | Lemmings pop out of their holes. Tap them to tickle them, but don't boop Grizzly! |
| **Match** | Flip the cards and find the 6 matching pairs. |
| **Count** | Lemmings march in. Tap each one to count it, then pick how many there are. 5 rounds. |
| **Add** | Two groups of lemmings walk in under a number sentence like `3 + 2 = ?`. Pick the answer and the groups join up. 5 rounds, answers up to 10. |
| **Take Away** | Grizzly lines up his honey pots, then sneaky lemmings run off with some. Answer `5 − 2 = ?` (how many are left). 5 rounds. |
| **Maze** | Drag Grizzly through a hedge maze to his honey. Mazes grow from 5×4 to 9×6. |
| **Simon Says** | Grizzly drums a pattern on four drums; repeat it. Patterns grow from 2 to 7 drums. |
| **Bridge** | Drag numbered logs to fill the river gap exactly so the lemmings can cross (secretly adding). |
| **Sudoku** | A 4×4 picture sudoku with honey, apples, berries and fish. Four puzzles that get harder. |
| **Honey Shop** | Pay for each lemming's treat with 1, 2 and 5 coins. Later customers add twists like "exactly 2 coins". |
| **Leap** | Tap to jump over logs and rocks, hold for a big jump, and run all the way to Grizzly. |
| **Rafts** | Steer Grizzly's raft down the river to rescue 15 lemmings while dodging rocks. |
| **Stack** | Drop swinging honey pots to build a tower 8 pots tall. |

- No game over and no timers, so it's never frustrating
- **Play time for grown-ups:** 15 minutes a day unless you change it. Tap the ⚙️ on the menu, answer the grown-up sum, and pick Off, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes; you can also add 5 minutes or start today again. A clock counts down during games, and the sky turns from morning to night as time runs out. Grizzly says when he's getting sleepy, lets your child finish the current game or level, then falls asleep until tomorrow. Reopening the game doesn't reset it. For a lock that can't be got round, also use your tablet's Screen Time.
- Instructions and numbers are read aloud, for kids who can't read yet
- Finishing a game earns a star sticker on its menu card
- Every game has its own background tune, plus sound effects, all made in the browser with nothing to download.
  The music-note button (top right) turns just the music off; the speaker button turns off all sound

## How to play

**Play online:** https://hermannwwr-cmyk.github.io/Grizzly-and-the-lemmings-game/


Open `index.html` in any web browser. It works on tablets, phones and computers, with no install.
Turn a tablet sideways for the biggest view.

- **Touch / mouse:** tap things. In Catch, tap or drag where Grizzly should go.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys (or A / D) move Grizzly, `Enter` presses the big button,
  `Esc` goes back to the menu, `M` turns sound on/off.
